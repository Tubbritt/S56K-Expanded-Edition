## Understand the risk

S56K Expanded Edition is an independent modification for vintage AKAI S5000 and S6000 samplers. Writing firmware to the sampler's flash memory changes its installed operating system. An interrupted or unsuccessful update, incompatible hardware, failing media, power loss, or an undiscovered software fault may leave the sampler unable to start or operate correctly. Recovery may require specialist equipment, repair, replacement parts or service, and may not be possible.

Loading firmware temporarily and obtaining a successful result do not guarantee that a later flash operation will succeed. Testing, checksums and retained backup files reduce some risks; they do not make an update risk-free or provide automatic recovery.

You are responsible for deciding whether to use the firmware and updater, checking the applicable model and release instructions, verifying downloaded files, backing up programs, samples and configuration, using suitable media, USB connections and a reliable power supply, and determining whether you have the knowledge and resources needed to proceed. Assign a USB driver only to the correctly identified sampler; changing a driver association may affect other software that uses that device. Do not interrupt an update once flash writing has started. If you are uncertain about the process or cannot accept the possibility of loss or repair costs, do not install it.
